Nature-based solutions
create value.
How do we measure it?

From mountains to sea, nature-based solutions can reduce risk and support ecosystems and communities. This hub helps turn their potential benefits into questions we can explore, evaluate, and use.

01 / The challenge

What is the problem?

Water, ecosystems, and communities are connected. Decisions are harder when we cannot see how risks and benefits move across that whole landscape.

02 / The approach

Why nature-based solutions?

Restoring and working with natural systems may address more than one challenge at once. The question is where an approach fits—and what it can realistically deliver.

03 / The value

What counts as value?

Flood reduction is only part of the picture. Ecological health, cultural connections, and community well-being also matter when judging outcomes.

04 / The evidence

How can we evaluate it?

We bring together indicators, research, and local context to examine what changes, for whom, and with what degree of confidence.

05 / The hub

How does it work?

Explore places and interventions, then connect them to case studies, assessment approaches, and practical guidance.

06 / The decision

What can we do with the results?

Use the evidence to ask better questions about planning, investment, stewardship, and long-term monitoring.
