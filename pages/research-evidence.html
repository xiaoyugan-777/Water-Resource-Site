About this data

The explorer counts unique papers in the Hawaiʻi NbS Hub case-study collection. Choose a place and two study attributes to explore their intersections; select a count to read the papers behind it.

A paper may belong to several categories, so totals are not additive. Papers without both selected attributes remain in the collection but are not represented in that matrix view. NbS and ecosystem labels include provisional classifications; consult the original studies for context.
