About the Hawaiʻi NbS Hub

The Hawaiʻi Nature-based Solutions (NbS) Hub brings together maps, published research, and monitoring guides for people caring for Hawaiʻi’s forests, streams, and coasts.

Our team at the Oleson Lab, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, works with partners to understand what restoration can achieve, who benefits, and how to recognize change over time. Meet our team and project partners.

What counts as value?

A community restoring a loko iʻa (Hawaiian fishpond) might track how much food it produces, whether native fish are returning, or how often families gather there to learn from one another. Each observation answers a different question about what the restoration makes possible.

Across the hub, we consider flood risk alongside ecological health, cultural connections, and community well-being. The people who care for a place help define which outcomes matter there and what evidence would show progress.

How to use the hub

  1. Start with a place. Find your island on the Hawaiʻi map and explore the landscape around your site.
  2. Find relevant evidence. Filter the Case Study Explorer by island, ecosystem, or restoration approach. To compare research across settings, select a count in Research Evidence to see the papers behind it.
  3. Choose what to observe. Bring one question from your project to the Monitoring & Evaluation toolkit. Use it to choose an indicator and decide how to share what you learn.
