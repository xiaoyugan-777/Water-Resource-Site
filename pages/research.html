Partners

Our partners

InEvalTool is built on deep relationships across Hawaii and the Pacific. Our partners bring the scientific expertise, community ties, and institutional reach that make rigorous, equitable NBS evaluation possible.

Technical partners

Technical partners

Scientists, hydrologists, economists, and engineers who provide the data, models, and methods that power InEvalTool's evaluation frameworks. These partners ensure the toolkit is grounded in the best available science adapted for island constraints.

Their role in NBS

Provide physical, ecological, and economic evidence of NBS performance, modeling flood attenuation, water quality, biodiversity outcomes, and ecosystem service value.

Their role in this project

Co-develop evaluation methods, advise on data and modeling tools, guide indicator selection, and support scientific reporting across the toolkit.

Strategic partners

Strategic partners

Community organizations, government agencies, and regional networks who bring place-based knowledge, frontline relationships, and institutional reach. These partners ground InEvalTool in Hawaiian values and ensure the toolkit serves the communities who need it most.

Their role in NBS

Connect evaluation to policy, funding, and on-the-ground implementation so that NBS outcomes can inform real planning and investment decisions.

Their role in this project

Help define priority use cases, shape user needs, identify decision contexts, and strengthen pathways for implementation across Hawaii.

aina group

aina group

Community-rooted and place-based collaborators who bring cultural knowledge, stewardship practice, and lived relationships into the toolkit.

Their role in NBS

Center aloha aina, stewardship, and place-based practice so evaluation reflects more than technical indicators alone.

Their role in this project

Ground the toolkit in local context, cultural meaning, and the realities of community-led restoration and care.
