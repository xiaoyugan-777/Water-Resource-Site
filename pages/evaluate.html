The evaluation process

1

Specify climate risks

Identify flooding, drought, and compound hazards the community faces

2

Map the system

Physical, ecological, social, cultural, and economic relationships

3

Build theory of change

Trace causal links from NBS interventions to desired outcomes

4

Define results chains

Identify indicators and monitoring pathways

5

Select holistic indicators

Elevate local and Indigenous knowledge alongside scientific metrics

6

Assess and value outcomes

Ecosystem service valuation, physical and ecological modeling

7

Apply decision tools

Cost-benefit analysis and multi-criteria decision analysis

8

Share and disseminate

Contribute evidence to the database and community of practice
