About Oleson Lab

The Oleson Ecological Economics Lab is part of the Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Management at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. We study how decisions about forests, freshwater, and coastal waters affect the people who depend on them.

Oleson Lab websiteolesonlab.org

Principal investigator

Kirsten Oleson

Professor of Ecological Economics

Kirsten studies how communities and public agencies weigh the benefits of protecting nature. Her work combines economics, ecology, and decision science to examine choices about food security, marine conservation, and climate adaptation in Hawaiʻi and across the Pacific.
