All our topics

Begin with the work already underway and the questions your team is already asking.

Observation, shared learning, and practical decision-making are at the heart of monitoring and evaluation.

Choose the question that best describes what your group is working on.

Across Hawaiʻi, communities and organizations are caring for streams, forests, wetlands, fishponds, and other places.

Everyday restoration work already includes observing change, addressing problems, sharing knowledge, and deciding what to do next.

This learning can happen during field work, staff meetings, conversations with kūpuna and community members, and everyday observations of ʻāina.

Monitoring and Evaluation can build on the work your team is already doing.
